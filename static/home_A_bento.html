FAQ

Common questions

How fast will I get my video?

Most orders are delivered within 24 hours of you approving the script and photo. Cinematic (Veo3) videos can take a bit longer, usually 20–30 minutes for generation plus review time.

What do I need to send to get started?

Just one clear photo of your product. We'll write the script, generate the voiceover, and build the video from there — you can request changes before final delivery.

What languages are supported?

Hindi, English, and Hinglish voiceovers are all available at no extra cost.

Do you offer ongoing social media posting too?

Yes — our AI Social Studio can auto-post branded content to Instagram and Facebook daily. Ask us about combining it with video ads.

Is there a subscription or is it pay-per-video?

Video ads are pay-per-video, no subscription required. Some services like AI Chat Assistant and Voice Reception run as a monthly service — talk to us for pricing on those.
